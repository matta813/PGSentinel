# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting corrections.
- Protect private operational data shared during support and review.
- Give credit and make space for different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository activity, Discussions, issue and pull request interactions, and public representation of the project. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary and proportionate.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/matta813). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
