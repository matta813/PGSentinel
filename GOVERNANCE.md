# Governance

PGSentinel is currently maintainer-led. The maintainer is responsible for roadmap decisions, reviews, releases, security response, and repository administration.

Decisions favor operational safety, explainable findings, low monitoring overhead, and maintainability. Significant changes should begin with an issue or Discussion and document alternatives and tradeoffs. Consensus is preferred; when it cannot be reached, the maintainer makes and records the final decision.

Contributors gain broader responsibility through sustained, constructive work and sound review judgment. Future maintainers may be added publicly through a governance change. Releases follow [docs/releases.md](docs/releases.md), and security matters follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
